OFFLINE DAOC - QUICK COMMANDS AND LAUNCHER SHORTCUTS

On the launcher, click + Lv.1 or + Lv.50 under a faction:
  Normal click: +1 bot
  Hold Shift while clicking: +10 bots
  Hold Ctrl while clicking: +100 bots

/tele X
  X = a gamebot's name. Teleports you to that bot.
  You can also right-click bot names on the launcher to teleport.

/mobs X
  X = a level. Shows names of mobs of that level.

/tele mob X
  X = the mob's exact name. Teleports you to a spawn.
  For dungeon mobs, the configured entrance approach is used where applicable.

/tc
  Teleports you to your realm's Realm Exchange NPC.

/grind
  Starts automated grinding with a companion bot group, including for AFK use.

/pull
  Orders your companion group and pets to engage your selected enemy. When possible,
  a tank makes first contact before the rest of the group joins the fight.

/train <line> <level>
  Trains a specialization to the chosen level using your available specialization
  points. Select a valid trainer for your class first.

/spawn
  Opens a menu of valid companion bots to summon.

/spawn X
  X = a class name, if you prefer not to use the menu or want a macro.

/raid 40
/raid 80
  Enables a 40- or 80-member companion raid. Use BEFORE /spawn.
  Requires level 50. The total includes your own character.

/aggressive
  Companions automatically attack what you attack and react defensively.

/defensive
  Companions engage nearby threats while staying near you, letting you pull mobs
  back to the group. Useful for harder content.

These controls apply to your companions, not autonomous gamebots.
/spawn 40 and /spawn 80 are not raid-size commands; use /raid 40 or /raid 80.
See docs/PLAY.md for setup and docs/LLM-QUICKSTART.md for modifying your own version.
ALL SERVER COMMANDS.txt is the exhaustive advanced reference, not this quick guide.
